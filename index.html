The Cosmic Egg

A pendant lamp after the Orphic egg.

A silver serpent wraps an egg of frosted glass three times, then climbs the cable in a tightening spiral. When the egg is lit, every coil catches its light.

2.95 metres of serpent

38 mm across at its thickest, tapering to a tail that coils around the cable.

240 engraved motifs

Stars, rosettes, sprigs, sunbursts, crescents and spirals. No two segments match.

One warm bulb

A frosted egg, 240 by 320 mm, glowing at 2700 K from a single E27 LED.

Every coil is engraved.

240 motifs are cut 0.55 mm into the serpent's skin, then filled with a dark wash so they read against the chrome.

It begins as twenty printed parts.

Sixteen serpent segments, three diffuser shells and a cover. Each fits a 256 mm print bed, and every segment seats on the egg with a shaped saddle and two steel dowels.

From grey plastic to mirror chrome.

  1. PrintedGrey PLA, straight off the bed.
  2. PrimedFilled and sanded to 1000 grit.
  3. Gloss blackThe base the chrome will mirror.
  4. Mirror chromeMist coats until the black turns to metal.
  5. WashedA dark wash settles into the engraving.

75 cm above the table.

The serpent's snout is the lamp's lowest point. It hangs 750 mm over the tabletop: low enough to light the meal, high enough to see across it.
