the idea

The serpent and the egg

The Orphic egg is the cosmos at the moment of creation: an egg with a serpent coiled around it. Jacob Bryant printed it as an engraved plate in his Analysis of Ancient Mythology (1774), where the serpent stands for the creative spirit that warms the egg into life.

This lamp takes the image literally. The egg is the diffuser. The serpent wraps it three times, then leaves the glass and rises in a tightening spiral, its tail curling around the cable. Its head breaks away at the bottom and points out over the table.

Its skin carries 240 engraved motifs: stars, rosettes, leaf sprigs, sunbursts, crescents and spirals. They are generated, so no two segments repeat, and they are cut 0.55 mm deep into the printed parts, deep enough to hold a dark wash.

in the room

Over the table

A dining pendant hangs with its lowest point 700 to 850 mm above the tabletop: low enough to pool light on the table, high enough to see the person opposite. The serpent's snout is the lowest point of this lamp, and it sits at 750 mm.

  • 750 mmSnout to tabletop. Adjust at the canopy's cord grip.
  • 297 mmOverall width. Suits a table 90–100 cm wide, roughly three times the lamp.
  • 4–6 seatsOne lamp for a table up to 200 cm. Above that, hang two, 80–90 cm apart.
  • ceiling − 1850Visible cable length in mm. At a 2700 mm ceiling that is 850 mm.
  • ≤ 8 W LEDAbout 800 lm, 2200–2700 K. Enough for the table without glare through the frosted shell.

maintenance

Changing the bulb

The bottom of the egg is a bayonet cap. It opens to a 120 mm hole, wide enough for a G95 bulb and a hand.

  1. Switch off at the wall and let the bulb cool for a few minutes.
  2. Hold the cap from below, turn it 25° anticlockwise (seen from below) and lower it. Three lugs leave their slots.
  3. Unscrew the old bulb through the opening and screw in the new one. E27, LED only, no more than 8 W.
  4. Lift the cap into the collar, turn it 25° clockwise until it drops 1 mm into the lock, and let go.

Safety

Have a qualified electrician make the mains connections at the canopy and the socket. Use double-insulated cable and a cord grip at both ends; the cable carries the whole lamp. Fix the canopy to a ceiling hook or box rated for at least 10 kg. LED bulbs only: PETG softens around 75 °C.

The build files

The STL print files, the Blender project and the scripts are not part of this preview; they will be available to buy. Everything is generated from one parametric script, so a change to a dimension in lamp_geometry.py rebuilds the model, the renders and every print file to match.
